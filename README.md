# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

**💡 Purpose**

This project provides a comprehensive analysis of over 150,000 e-commerce transactions from 2021 to 2025, exploring key performance indicators in sales, customer behavior, product performance, and profitability to drive data-informed decisions.

--- 
**💻 Tech Stack**

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
 * Tableau- Data visualization, interactive dashboard creation, data sorting, and preparation for analysis.
 * Data Preparation - Data transformation, cleaning, sorting, and joins performed entirely within Tableau.
---

**📖 Data Source**

The dataset for this project was obtained from Kaggle. You can access and explore the data here: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/datascikhan/e-commerce-sales-and-customer-analytics.

--- 

**📌 Key Features & Highlights**
 * 📊 Sales by Product Category: Visualizing total sales across various product categories to identify top-performing product lines.
 * 📈 Monthly Net Sales Trend: Displaying the monthly net sales trend over time to uncover seasonal patterns and growth trajectories.
 * 💰 Net Sales by Profit Analysis: Analyzing the relationship between net sales and profit margins through detailed scatter plots.
 * 🌍 Sales by Location: An interactive geographic map showing sales distribution across different regions like the USA, Australia, and India, allowing users to filter and drill down into specific regional performance.
--- 

**💡 Insights & Business Opportunities**

This dashboard helps stakeholders identify high-potential expansion regions, recognize popular item types, and optimize marketing and pricing strategies based on deeper customer insights and purchasing trends.
